Formats & rules

Locked in: Wolf on Friday, Cha Cha Cha on Saturday. Tap a section to open it. Scoring is on handicap points, full handicap, and every point goes straight into the team's tour total.

🔢Points per holeStableford · full handicap · par = 2

Everyone plays off the handicap on the Players tab, the same at Randpark and Jackal Creek (the two courses rate almost the same). Take your handicap shots off your score on the hole, then:

Net score on the holePoints
Double bogey or worse0
Bogey (1 over)1
Par2
Birdie (1 under)3
Eagle (2 under)4
Albatross (3 under)5

Example: par 4, you get a shot on the hole and score 5. Net 4 = par = 2 points. A blow-up hole costs you nothing below 0, so pick it up and get to the next tee.

🐺WolfFriday · Randpark · within each fourball

One player per hole is the Wolf. He tees off first and decides how brave (or stupid) he's feeling. Holes 1 and 10 are looseners with no Wolf and no points. They're for the gees, but fines still apply. Over the other 16 holes everyone gets to be Wolf 4 times, in an order the Wolf wheel picks before we tee off.

4pts

Blind Wolf

Call before tee shot

Wolf alone vs the 2 opponents. His teammate is neutral. Win: 4 to his team. Lose: 4 to theirs.

3pts

Lone Wolf

Call after tee shot

Same as Blind, but he's seen where his drive went first. Win: 3. Lose: 3 to the other lot.

1+1pts

Partner Wolf

Pick after 2nd or 3rd tee shot

Picks any one of the other 3, his teammate or one of the other team (confirmed). Don't pick and you get the 4th player. Combined scores decide it. The winning pair get 1 point each, so 2 for the team if he picked his teammate.

Blind and Lone Wolf must beat both opponents: his score has to be better than each of the other team's two scores. Ties: nobody scores. Partner from the other team = "damage control", both teams get 1 and it's all square.

🎡The Wolf wheelFriday · each fourball spins before teeing off

Each fourball spins once. The wheel picks the first Wolf, then who leads for the other team. After that it alternates: Team A, Team B, Team A's other player, Team B's other player, and round again. Holes 2–9 and 11–18 follow that order.

The rich list
    How the Bookie works
    • Everyone on Tour V starts with R1,000,000 of fake money. Past tourists can buy in for their own million. It isn't real. Nobody pays anybody.
    • You bet against the house, like Betway. Each answer has odds: 2.50 means R10k pays R25k if it lands. Your odds lock in the moment you bet.
    • Steve's the bookie: he sets the odds and can shorten or lengthen them any time (only new bets get the new odds). The bar behind each answer shows where the money's going.
    • You can bet more than once, on anything, until betting closes. Question gets scrapped = everyone gets their stake back.
    • Pitch a market for R50,000. Steve declines it = money gone. Steve approves it = your R50k becomes your opening bet at maker's odds: double the opening odds on your answer.
    • Steve posts the answers. Most money at the end of the weekend wins.
