Formats & rules

Locked in: Wolf on Friday, Cha Cha Cha on Saturday. The weekend plan is on the Schedule tab. Scoring is on handicap points, full handicap, and every point goes straight into the team's tour total.

How a hole is scored · standard Stableford, full handicap

Points per hole

Everyone plays off the handicap on the Players tab, the same at Randpark and Jackal Creek (the two courses rate almost the same). Take your handicap shots off your score on the hole, then:

Net score on the holePoints
Double bogey or worse0
Bogey (1 over)1
Par2
Birdie (1 under)3
Eagle (2 under)4
Albatross (3 under)5

Example: par 4, you get a shot on the hole and score 5. Net 4 = par = 2 points. A blow-up hole costs you nothing below 0, so pick it up and get to the next tee.

Day 1 · Fri, Randpark · within each fourball

Wolf

One player per hole is the Wolf. He tees off first and decides how brave (or stupid) he's feeling. Holes 1 and 10 are looseners with no Wolf and no points. They're for the gees, but fines still apply. Over the other 16 holes everyone gets to be Wolf 4 times, in an order the Wolf wheel picks before we tee off.

6pts

Blind Wolf

Call before tee shot

Wolf alone vs the 2 opponents. His teammate is neutral. Win: 6 to his team. Lose: 6 to theirs.

4pts

Lone Wolf

Call after tee shot

Same as Blind, but he's seen where his drive went first. Win: 4. Lose: 4 to the other lot.

2+2pts

Partner Wolf

Pick after 2nd or 3rd tee shot

Picks any one of the other 3, his teammate or one of the other team (confirmed). Don't pick and you get the 4th player. Combined scores decide it. Win or lose: 2 points each.

Blind and Lone Wolf must beat both opponents: his score has to be better than each of the other team's two scores. Ties: nobody scores. Partner from the other team = "damage control", both teams get 2 and it's all square.

Friday · spin before you tee off

The Wolf wheel

Each fourball spins once. The wheel picks the first Wolf, then who leads for the other team. After that it alternates: Team A, Team B, Team A's other player, Team B's other player, and round again. Holes 2–9 and 11–18 follow that order.
