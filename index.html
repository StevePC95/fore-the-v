Formats & rules

Locked in: Wolf on Friday, Cha Cha Cha on Saturday. The execs are still finalising a few details (see Exec Qs). Scoring is on handicap points, full handicap, and every point goes straight into the team's tour total.

Day 1 · Fri, Randpark · within each fourball

Wolf

One player per hole is the Wolf. He tees off first and decides how brave (or stupid) he's feeling. Holes 1 and 10 are looseners with no Wolf, so over the other 16 holes everyone gets to be Wolf 4 times.

6pts

Blind Wolf

Call before tee shot

Wolf alone vs the 2 opponents. His teammate is neutral. Win: 6 to his team. Lose: 6 to theirs.

4pts

Lone Wolf

Call after tee shot

Same as Blind, but he's seen where his drive went first. Win: 4. Lose: 4 to the other lot.

2+2pts

Partner Wolf

Pick after 2nd or 3rd tee shot

Picks any one of the other 3, his teammate or one of the other team (confirmed). Don't pick and you get the 4th player. Combined scores decide it. Win or lose: 2 points each.

Ties: nobody scores. Partner from the other team = "damage control", both teams get 2 and it's all square.

Day 2 · Sat, Jackal Creek · all 4 vs all 4, across both fourballs

The rules
  • Friday night runs from walking off the 18th until 02:00. Double spirit or 500ml beer = 1. Down-down = 2. Cap: 10 points a man.
